### About Me

Since 2021 I have been working in IT on Linux administration, infrastructure automation, and high availability: DevOps, SRE, SysAdmin, and Support L2–L3.

I keep platforms up, make recurring failures visible, and help neighboring teams automate routine work.

> Metrics, a post-mortem, and a page in Confluence should make the same incident harder to repeat.

### Operations

- Fleet of about **500 virtual machines** (Proxmox, ROSA): administration, support, and day-to-day reliability.
- Operational flow of **20+ incidents and requests a day** in Jira.
- Recurring issues cut down with new metrics, written post-mortems, and fuller Confluence docs.
- Routine work in neighboring teams moved into automation so the same steps are not done by hand.

Focus of the job: scaling and support of **PostgreSQL / Postgres Pro** and **Redis**, plus the path in front of them — **Kubernetes**, **Docker**, **HAProxy**, **Nginx** — and the view of the system through **Prometheus**, **Grafana**, **Zabbix**, **Graylog**, and **OpenTelemetry**.
